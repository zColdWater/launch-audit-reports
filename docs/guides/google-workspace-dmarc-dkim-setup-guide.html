Google Workspace SPF, 2048-Bit DKIM & DMARC Setup Guide (2026)

New Google Workspace domains ship with DKIM disabled by default. Turning on 2048-bit DKIM and pairing it with clean SPF + DMARC is the fastest way to protect cold outreach and team email deliverability.

1. Publish a Clean Google Workspace SPF Record

Add include:_spf.google.com to a single apex TXT record. Remember Google Workspace uses 3–4 DNS lookups internally, leaving 6–7 lookups for other tools.

TXT  @   "v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~all"

2. Enable 2048-Bit DKIM in Google Admin Console

Go to Admin Console → Apps → Google Workspace → Gmail → Authenticate email, generate a 2048-bit key for selector google, add the google._domainkey TXT record in DNS, and click Start authentication.

3. Add DMARC & Verify Automatically

Publish _dmarc and verify everything live with InboxShield before launching outreach sequences.

TXT  _dmarc   "v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; pct=100; rua=mailto:dmarc@yourdomain.com"
