Resend Emails Going to Spam? 2026 SPF, DKIM, DMARC & Return-Path Fix Checklist

Most Resend spam-folder drops come from sending on a root domain with broken SPF lookup counts, missing DMARC alignment, or sharing reputation between marketing blasts and auth emails.

1. Isolate Transactional Mail on a Dedicated Subdomain

Verify a dedicated subdomain such as mail.yourdomain.com or send.yourdomain.com in Resend rather than mixing cold outreach and OTP emails on your apex domain. Resend uses Amazon SES infrastructure under the hood and sets a custom Return-Path (send.<domain>) for SPF alignment.

# Recommended DNS records for dedicated transactional subdomain (mail.yourdomain.com)
MX   send.mail.yourdomain.com   feedback-smtp.us-east-1.amazonses.com (priority 10)
TXT  send.mail.yourdomain.com   "v=spf1 include:amazonses.com ~all"
TXT  resend._domainkey.mail     "p=MIGfMA0GCSqGSIb3DQEBAQUAA4GNADCBiQ..."

2. Audit Your Root SPF Record Against the 10-Lookup Cap

Even if Resend passes DKIM, an apex SPF record exceeding RFC 7208's 10-DNS-lookup limit triggers PermError at Gmail and Microsoft 365. Count nested includes across Google Workspace (_spf.google.com = 3–4 lookups), HubSpot, Zendesk, and SendGrid.

3. Publish a Valid DMARC Record (Required by Gmail & Yahoo)

Gmail and Yahoo reject or spam-fold unauthenticated bulk senders lacking a _dmarc TXT record. Start at p=none with an aggregate report mailbox (rua=), verify 99%+ DKIM/SPF alignment for 7 days, then enforce p=quarantine or p=reject.

TXT  _dmarc.yourdomain.com   "v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; pct=100; adkim=r; aspf=r; rua=mailto:dmarc@yourdomain.com"

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do my Resend password reset emails land in Gmail Spam even though Resend says Verified?

Domain verification in Resend only confirms the DKIM and Return-Path records Resend gave you. It does not guarantee your apex domain has a valid DMARC record, stays under 10 SPF lookups, or avoids shared IP/content filters.

Should I put include:amazonses.com on my root domain for Resend?

Only if you send directly from the root domain's Return-Path. Resend defaults to a send.* Return-Path subdomain so your root SPF lookup budget stays lean.
