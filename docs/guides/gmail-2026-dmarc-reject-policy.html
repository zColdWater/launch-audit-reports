Gmail & Yahoo 2026 DMARC Enforcement: Safe Rollout from p=none to p=reject

Over 40% of growing SaaS domains still leave DMARC stuck at p=none or missing completely, leaving them vulnerable to brand spoofing and strict Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender penalties.

1. Stage 1 — Visibility (7–14 Days at p=none with rua=)

Inventory every system sending mail as your domain (Google Workspace, Resend/Postmark, Stripe, HubSpot, Zendesk, Intercom) and publish a monitoring record so mailbox providers send daily XML alignment reports.

TXT  _dmarc.yourdomain.com   "v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:dmarc-reports@yourdomain.com; adkim=r; aspf=r"

2. Stage 2 — Gradual Quarantine (pct=25 -> pct=100)

Once every legitimate sender passes DKIM alignment on your own domain (custom DKIM CNAMEs configured in every SaaS tool), switch to p=quarantine; pct=25 for 48 hours, then pct=100.

TXT  _dmarc.yourdomain.com   "v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; pct=100; rua=mailto:dmarc-reports@yourdomain.com"

3. Stage 3 — Full Spoofing Protection (p=reject + sp=reject)

Lock down both your apex domain and unused subdomains with p=reject; sp=reject. Add _mta-sts for TLS encryption enforcement and run continuous CI checks so marketing never adds an unaligned sender silently.

TXT  _dmarc.yourdomain.com   "v=DMARC1; p=reject; sp=reject; pct=100; rua=mailto:dmarc-reports@yourdomain.com"

Frequently Asked Questions

Will switching to DMARC p=reject break Google Workspace or Resend?

Not if DKIM signing is enabled in Google Admin and custom DKIM records are verified in Resend. DMARC passes as long as EITHER aligned SPF or aligned DKIM passes.

What happens to subdomains if I omit sp= in my DMARC record?

Subdomains inherit the root p= policy automatically unless overridden by sp= or their own _dmarc.sub record.
