AI Crawler robots.txt Guide (2026): GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot & PerplexityBot

Blocking OAI-SearchBot or PerplexityBot in robots.txt makes your SaaS invisible in AI search answers. Learn the exact user-agent split between search citation bots and training bots.

1. Understand Search Citation Bots vs. Training Bots

OpenAI separates OAI-SearchBot (used to surface live citations in ChatGPT Search) from GPTBot (model training). Perplexity uses PerplexityBot for search indexing, and Anthropic uses ClaudeBot and Claude-SearchBot.

# Recommended SaaS robots.txt: allow AI search discovery & link sitemap
User-agent: *
Allow: /

User-agent: OAI-SearchBot
Allow: /

User-agent: PerplexityBot
Allow: /

User-agent: ClaudeBot
Allow: /

User-agent: GPTBot
Allow: /

Sitemap: https://yourdomain.com/sitemap.xml

2. Want Citations Without Allowing Training?

If legal policy requires blocking training crawlers while keeping ChatGPT Search & Perplexity referral traffic, disallow only GPTBot while explicitly allowing OAI-SearchBot and PerplexityBot.

User-agent: GPTBot
Disallow: /

Allow: /
