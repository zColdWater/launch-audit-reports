3. 1. What Actually Breaks Cold & Transactional Deliverability in 2026

Since Google and Yahoo tightened bulk-sender authentication enforcement, most startup and SMB email drops are caused by four concrete DNS misconfigurations rather than mysterious inbox algorithms: exceeding RFC 7208's strict 10-DNS-lookup limit in SPF (silent PermError), publishing duplicate v=spf1 records, leaving _dmarc missing or stuck on p=none, or rotating an ESP without verifying the 2048-bit DKIM selector.

Both EasyDMARC and InboxShield catch these exact records. The practical difference is workflow and cost: EasyDMARC prices around ongoing enterprise XML log ingestion ($36–$72+/mo), whereas 80% of founders and agencies mainly need fast pre-launch verification, an SPF Flattener when their SaaS stack crosses 9–10 lookups, and clear registrar-specific DNS steps to fix the root cause in 5 minutes.

  • Real live benchmark: Auditing www.apple.com on InboxShield auto-resolves apple.com in under 0.6s, inspecting MX, SPF recursion (2/10), 24 DKIM selectors, DMARC p=quarantine, and 5 DNSBL lists simultaneously.
  • Real live benchmark: Auditing github.com flags its 10/10 SPF lookup cliff and expands its nested includes in /spf-flattener so operators can inspect exact CIDR counts before adding another ESP.
  • Zero wrong-provider SPF copy-paste: Auditing qq.com or feishu.cn identifies Tencent Exmail or Feishu Mail and outputs include:spf.mail.qq.com or include:_spf.feishu.cn instead of blindly recommending Google Workspace.
