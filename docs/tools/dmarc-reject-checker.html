Frequently Asked Questions

Will switching straight to p=reject break legitimate emails?

Only if an authorized service (such as CRM, billing, or support software) sends mail using your domain without valid aligned DKIM or SPF. Always start with p=none + rua= reporting, confirm all legitimate senders pass DKIM, then step up through p=quarantine to p=reject.
