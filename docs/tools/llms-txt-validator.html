Does /llms.txt replace robots.txt or sitemap.xml?

No. Keep both robots.txt (explicitly allowing OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, and PerplexityBot) and sitemap.xml. /llms.txt acts as a compact, Markdown-formatted table of contents designed specifically for LLM context windows.
