High-Intent Launch Troubleshooting Guides (2026)

Email Deliverability

Fix Resend Emails Going to Spam

Most Resend spam-folder drops come from sending on a root domain with broken SPF lookup counts, missing DMARC alignment, or sharing reputation between marketing blasts and auth emails.

 AI Search & GEO

Next.js llms.txt Generator Guide

An /llms.txt file gives AI search assistants a curated, noise-free Markdown map of your product, docs, pricing, and API endpoints without forcing crawlers to parse megabytes of client-side JS bundles.

 Email Deliverability

Gmail 2026 DMARC p=reject Rollout

Over 40% of growing SaaS domains still leave DMARC stuck at p=none or missing completely, leaving them vulnerable to brand spoofing and strict Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender penalties.

 Email Deliverability

Fix SPF >10 DNS Lookup PermError

RFC 7208 caps SPF evaluation at 10 DNS-querying mechanisms including nested includes. Crossing 11 lookups causes immediate SPF PermError across Gmail and Microsoft 365.

 AI Search & GEO

AI Crawler robots.txt Guide (2026)

Blocking OAI-SearchBot or PerplexityBot in robots.txt makes your SaaS invisible in AI search answers. Learn the exact user-agent split between search citation bots and training bots.

 Startup Naming

USPTO Class 9 & 42 Trademark Check

An available .ai or .io domain offers zero legal protection if a live federal USPTO registration exists in Nice Class 009 (downloadable apps/software) or Class 042 (SaaS/cloud platforms).

 Email Deliverability

Google Workspace SPF/DKIM/DMARC

New Google Workspace domains ship with DKIM disabled by default. Turning on 2048-bit DKIM and pairing it with clean SPF + DMARC is the fastest way to protect cold outreach and team email deliverability.

 Email Deliverability

Fix AWS SES / Postmark DMARC Alignment

DMARC does not pass just because SPF passes — the Return-Path domain (SPF) or d= signing domain (DKIM) must match the visible From: header domain.

 AI Search & GEO

SaaS JSON-LD Schema for AI Search

Over half of SaaS homepages ship zero structured JSON-LD data, forcing AI search models to guess pricing tiers and feature sets from unstructured marketing copy.

 Startup Naming

Avoid Domain Front-Running via RDAP

RDAP (Registration Data Access Protocol) is ICANN's official JSON replacement for port-43 WHOIS. Querying registry RDAP endpoints directly removes commercial middlemen when vetting startup names.
